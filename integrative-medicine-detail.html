吾月－婦產科

Treatment Overview 療程簡介

走出月子中心，妳以為一切終於重回正軌，卻發現自己陷入了一場名為「第五孕期」的漫長餘震。

荷爾蒙像是一場退潮，留下了斷電般的疲憊、莫名低落的情緒，以及那個代謝變慢、連自己都感到陌生的輪廓。常規檢查告訴妳「一切正常」，但只有妳知道，內在的微氣候早已失衡。這不是妳想太多，而是身體在經歷一場劇烈遷徙後，尚未找回定點。

「節律調理」不該只是補充，而是一場對身體秩序的溫柔校準。

我們從根源觀測那些微小的波動，幫妳校對生理的時差。當內在重新找回平衡，妳會發現，找回的不只是體態與膚質，更是那份對生活久違的掌控感。在吾青，我們陪妳度過這場轉變，讓妳在新的身份裡，依舊活得輕盈且純粹。

Stepping out of the postpartum care center, you thought everything was finally back on track, only to find yourself caught in the long aftershocks of what's known as the "fifth trimester."

Hormones recede like a tide, leaving behind a visceral exhaustion, inexplicable low moods, and a physique that feels unfamiliar even to yourself due to a slowed metabolism. Routine checkups tell you "everything's normal," but only you know that your internal microclimate has long been out of balance. This isn't just overthinking; your body, after a dramatic migration, hasn't yet found its footing.

"Rhythm regulation" shouldn't just be supplementation, but a gentle calibration of your body's order. We observe those subtle fluctuations at their root, helping you correct your physiological time difference. When your inner balance is restored, you'll find that you've regained not only your figure and skin, but also that long-lost sense of control over your life. At Wuqing, we accompany you through this transition, allowing you to live lightly and purely in your new identity.
